Invite integrity Flagged inviters

A pending invite only counts after a real-visitor signal (a chosen username plus a real session) and expires on its own after a week, so a drive-by invite-link bot earns nothing. Listed here are inviters whose pending invites still look automated. Investigate to see the pattern, then remove a flagged cluster. Active invites are never touched, every removal is logged, and a developer can undo it, so a clean inviter is never hurt by a mistake.

Mod applications Active members applying to help moderate

Someone who is in the Talkomatic Discord server is much likelier to be accepted, because we can reach them, and once approved they get the site mod role there so they can talk to the other moderators and the devs. Check the server before you decide.

Guide

Welcome to the moderation dashboard. Everything here is proven by your staff key and checked on the server, so nothing on this page can be faked from a browser. The dashboard keeps the team accountable to each other: every action is logged, visible to all staff, and permanent.

Your role The chain is enforced on the server: no one can act on someone at their level or above.
Junior mod (L1) PURPLE

Handles what is on screen, right now, in one room. Everything a junior can do is reversible.

  • Warn and kick from a room
  • Wipe what someone has typed, for everyone
  • Reset a bad username or location
  • Turn someone's profile picture off
  • Rename, lock, or slow a room, and clear the Talkoboard
  • Leave notes on users, and trace anyone in Activity
  • No bans, no IP blocks, cannot close a room
Full mod (L2) BLUE

Trusted juniors. They get the actions that outlast a single room and the review queues.

  • Every junior power
  • Kick and room ban
  • IP and identifier blocks up to 7 days, including a whole range
  • Close a room, spectate any room
  • Reports, appeals, applications, and the ban list
  • May grant a junior key; cannot promote anyone to L2
Developer RED

The people who run the site. Everything, on anyone except another developer.

  • Permanent blocks, and the only role that sees raw IPs
  • Grants, promotes, demotes, and revokes mod keys
  • Megaphone, ticker, party, spotlight, freeze
  • Feature flags, room size, maintenance, nuke
  • Key sessions, for spotting a shared or leaked key

The colors are the same everywhere. In a room a developer's row glows red, a full mod's blue, and a junior mod's purple, and the badge next to their name matches. Nobody can act on someone at their own level or above, and the server checks this on every single action, so a modified browser gains nothing.

The tabs

Activity

A live feed of every staff action and every identity event (sign-ins and name changes). Each card is one event, color coded by category. Search by name, action, IP, room, or message, and filter the feed by type.

Reports

Users flagged by the community, grouped by person and most reported first. The card header is the reported user; the list below is who reported them and why. Act right from the card: kick, or IP block for a set time. A reported user who goes offline can still be blocked using their last known address.

Applications

Active members applying for the junior role. Approve to mint and deliver their key automatically, or decline. Each card shows whether they have Discord linked; someone in the Talkomatic server is easier to trust and gets the site mod role once approved. Applications are never auto-accepted.

Ban list DEV

Every active block with a live countdown, who placed it, and the reason, grouped by person and searchable. One tap unbans. A permanent block is tagged in red.

Moderators DEV

The full staff roster, colored by rank: developers red, full mods blue, junior mods purple. Grant a new junior or full key (shown once), promote or demote a level, or revoke instantly with a live downgrade.

Sessions DEV

Who is connected on each staff key right now, plus every network a key has ever connected from. A key live on more than one network, or a brand-new network, may mean it was shared or leaked.

Good to know

Trace a user

Click any underlined name in the feed to focus that person. You will see every name they have used and every action taken against them, so a troublemaker can be followed even after a rename.

Leave a note

Type under any activity entry to add a permanent comment or ask why an action was taken. All staff see comments live, so use them to keep each other in the loop.

IP privacy

Raw IP addresses are shown to developers only. Moderators can still ban or IP block a user, because the server handles the address for them, but never see it.

Staff colors

Rank has one color everywhere, on this dashboard and in rooms: developers red, full mods (L2) blue, junior mods (L1) purple. In the Activity feed the person who acted is colored the same way, so you can tell at a glance who took an action.

Profile pictures

Any staff member can turn a user's Discord picture off from their row in a room. The block follows the browser rather than the session, so clearing cookies does not bring it back, and it can be lifted again from the same menu.

Activity categories The icon color on each Activity card tells you what kind of event it is.
